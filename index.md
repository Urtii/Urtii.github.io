I am a PhD student and Research Assistant in the
[Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering]({{ site.author.department_url }})
at [Hacettepe University](https://www.hacettepe.edu.tr/english/),
advised by [Assoc. Prof. İsmail Uyanık](https://www.ee.hacettepe.edu.tr/?link=400999&sublink=280&lang=e)
and a member of [NeuRoLab](https://neurolab.ee.hacettepe.edu.tr/).
My PhD is supported by the TÜBİTAK 2211-A National PhD Scholarship Programme —
see [Awards & Honors](/awards/) for the full list.

My research spans two interconnected areas: **LTP system identification** —
where I work on Floquet theory, harmonic transfer functions, and periodic
system analysis — and **robotics** — where I previously focused on
LiDAR-based odometry, 3D SLAM, and sensor fusion for quadruped platforms.
Alongside these, I contribute to neuromechanics research studying the
sensorimotor dynamics of weakly electric fish. See [Publications](/publications/) for papers.

I completed my M.S. at Hacettepe University in February 2025, with a thesis
on sensor fusion and mapping using 3D LiDAR odometry on a quadruped robot
platform, and spent part of that time as a Research Engineer with Turkcell's
5G VATS team, integrating LiDAR and stereo sensing on a quadruped robot.

Outside the lab, I build robotics side-projects for fun — a passive dynamic walker reconstruction, and
a self-balancing BallBot — mostly through my years with IEEE METU Robotics
and Automation Society, where I served as Vice President in 2018–2019. When
I'm not at a bench, I'm usually camping, diving (SSI AOWD), or out on the water.
